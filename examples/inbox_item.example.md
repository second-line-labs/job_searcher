# Example Bank AG - Head of IT Risk

Example Bank AG is a mid-size bank with its head office in Frankfurt and is supervised by the national banking supervisor. We are looking for a Head of IT Risk who reports to the Chief Risk Officer and leads a team of six people in the second line of defence.

In this role you own the ICT risk management framework of the bank and keep it in line with the Digital Operational Resilience Act and the national requirements for IT in banks. You set the risk appetite and the tolerances for ICT risk together with the management board and report on them every quarter.

You lead the risk assessments of critical applications and of the bank's important outsourcing arrangements, including its cloud providers. You challenge the first line on incidents, changes and projects, and you make sure that findings from audits and inspections are closed on time.

You are the contact for the supervisor and the external auditor on all questions of IT risk, and you prepare the bank for inspections. You develop your team and work closely with the Chief Information Security Officer and with the business continuity function.

You bring at least ten years of experience in IT risk or information security in a regulated financial institution, including several years in a leadership role. You know the regulatory requirements in detail and have dealt with a supervisor yourself. You speak English and German fluently and can explain technical risks to a board in plain words.

We offer a hybrid working model with two days a week in the office, a permanent contract and a pay package in line with the responsibility of the role.
